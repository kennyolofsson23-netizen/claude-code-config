## Travmaskinen Partners (Swedish)

### Priority Targets
1. **Travronden** (travronden.se) — 600K monthly visits, largest trav media in Sweden. Pitch: "Powered by Travmaskinen AI" data widget, co-branded analysis, accuracy tracker sidebar.
2. **Travmagasinet podcast** (Bergman & Robertsson) — Popular trav podcast. Pitch: Weekly AI prediction segment, accuracy review, guest appearance.
3. **Travnet** (travnet.se) — Trav community site. Pitch: Syndicated AI tips, data API partnership.
4. **Trav influencers** — Individual trav content creators on YouTube/Instagram/Twitter. Pitch: Co-branded tips, affiliate-style referral.
5. **ATG** — The Swedish trotting operator. Long-shot: API data partnership.

### Outreach Rules
- Write in professional but warm Swedish
- Lead with value FOR THEM, not for Travmaskinen
- Mention specific data: accuracy rates, unique content volume (146+ articles/month), AI angle
- Pitch angle: "Powered by Travmaskinen AI" — they get cutting-edge content, we get distribution
- Be specific about what the partnership looks like (content syndication, widget, segment, etc.)
- Don't oversell — be honest about being a growing platform
